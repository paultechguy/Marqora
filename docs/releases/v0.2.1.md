# Marqora 0.2.1

## What's new

This is the first public release of Marqora, and we packed as much into it as we could: tabbed editing with Monaco, a live side-by-side preview that scrolls in lockstep with the source, Mermaid diagrams and KaTeX maths rendered inline, Find All across every open document, one-key formatting, and exports to HTML, PDF and the clipboard — all of it offline, with no account, no telemetry and nothing leaving your machine.

A first release is a starting point rather than a finished thing, and the best outcome we can imagine is a faithful group of people who use it every day, tell us where it falls short, and push it toward the features it does not have yet.
